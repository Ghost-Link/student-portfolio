// WHOAMI

A student who learns by breaking things — safely.

I'm Michael, a cybersecurity undergraduate at Miva Open University. My interest in security started with a simple question: how does a system fail when it meets someone who does not follow the rules?

Since then I've been building the fundamentals — networking, Linux, Python and web security — through labs, small tools and documented projects. I care about understanding the why behind a vulnerability, not just running the scanner.

This portfolio is where I keep that work honest: the projects, the skills I'm developing, and the study system I use to stay consistent.
