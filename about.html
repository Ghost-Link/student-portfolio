// ABOUT ME

Curious about how systems fail.

I'm Michael Ewumi, a cybersecurity undergraduate who believes the best defenders understand both the technology and the people using it. Here's the background behind the work.

// PROFILE

Student, builder, lifelong learner.

My route into cybersecurity started with a stubborn question: why do systems break when they meet someone who ignores the rules? That curiosity took me from building small websites to deliberately studying networks, operating systems and the ways attackers think.

I'm currently studying for a BSc in Cybersecurity at Miva Open University. My learning is practical by design — I read the theory, then test it in a lab, then write down what surprised me. SentinelScan, PhishGuard and CampusShield are the result of that loop.

I'm most interested in defensive security: catching what others miss, reducing the blast radius when something goes wrong, and making security understandable to the people who have to live with it. I'm open to 2027 internships and junior roles where I can learn from a real team.
